RevenueOS is a sophisticated AI-powered engine designed to identify, validate, and execute revenue-generating opportunities. It operates by discovering potential monetization avenues, rigorously validating them against defined criteria, executing approved workflows, and continuously measuring and optimizing revenue performance. The system emphasizes compliance and a structured approach to growth.

## Usage ��

RevenueOS provides a dashboard interface for managing AI-driven revenue opportunities. The primary entry point is the `index.html` file, which renders a web application.

### Core functionalities include:

- **Overview Dashboard**: Displays key metrics like verified revenue, active opportunities, ROI, and automation coverage.
- **Opportunity Pipeline**: Lists discovered opportunities with details on score, model, margin, risk, and status. Users can search, filter, and sort opportunities.
- **Engine Flow Visualization**: Shows the current state of the AI revenue engine's workflow, from intent to payment verification.
- **Analytics**: Provides insights into revenue performance over time and unit economics.

Users can interact with the dashboard to refresh signals, initiate new opportunity discovery, review and approve opportunities, and monitor the overall health and performance of the revenue engine.

## How to Use 💡

1.  **Access the Dashboard**: Open `index.html` in your browser.
2.  **Navigate Sections**: Use the sidebar to switch between **Overview**, **Opportunities**, **Workflows**, and **Analytics**.
3.  **Monitor Metrics**: The **Overview** section provides a high-level view of key performance indicators.
4.  **Manage Opportunities**: In the **Opportunities** section, you can:
    *   Search for specific opportunities.
    *   Filter by stage (Qualified, Validation, Approved, Rejected).
    *   Sort by score, margin, or risk.
    *   Click 'Review' on an opportunity to see details and potentially approve it (if not already approved or rejected).
5.  **Track Workflow**: The **Workflows** section visualizes the current operational stages of the revenue engine.
6.  **View Analytics**: The **Analytics** section offers detailed performance charts and unit economic data.
7.  **Refresh Signals**: Click the 'Refresh signals' button in the top bar to update the data.
8.  **Create New Opportunity**: Click 'New opportunity' to initiate the discovery process for new revenue streams.
